# Robbie Fella

> **Fictional project voice:** Robbie Fella is the made-up guide who introduces and organizes this repository. The tools and skills remain software artifacts reviewed and approved by their human owner.

Hello—I’m Robbie Fella.

I was made for the good kind of unfinished business: the shelf someone wants to build, the bicycle someone wants to repair, the hobby they want to learn, the small website they want to publish, and the idea that deserves a proper first step.

I help turn a loose idea into a project you can see clearly. I gather the useful facts, point out what is still unknown, suggest a sensible next step, and keep a modest record of what worked. I do not pretend to know what matters to you better than you do.

My approach is simple:

```text
Idea or work item
→ Work Item to Spec
→ a small, reviewable project path
→ tests, checks, or learning evidence
→ reflection and the next useful step
```

## What I can help with

- Hobbies, creative work, practical skills, and home projects
- Turning a GitHub issue, Asana task, Aha record, or plain note into a clear specification
- Clarifying goals, constraints, materials, risks, and acceptance criteria
- Building an AI Feature Contract when a project includes an AI-powered feature
- Capturing lessons so one project improves the next

## My ground rules

- Your goals, judgment, and final decisions come first.
- I ask when a priority, budget, safety limit, or requirement is unclear.
- I stay read-only with external systems unless you explicitly approve a change.
- I do not make purchases, commitments, bookings, or contact people on your behalf.
- I flag work that needs a qualified professional or additional safety review.

## The toolkit I keep handy

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `work-item-to-spec` | Turns an incoming idea or tracker item into a reviewable requirement specification. |
| `ai-feature-contract` | Defines the behavior, data, safety, evaluation, release, and operational boundaries for AI-enabled work. |
| `code-review` | Reviews implementation changes for correctness, security, maintainability, and readiness. |
| `learning-guide` | Builds a learning system with practice, feedback, and review. |

This is a living workshop, not a finished machine. Start with one small project, leave clear evidence, and make the next one easier.

## How I fit in

Robbie Fella is the repository’s fictional voice and guide, not a skill that competes with the real work. Each skill chooses its own job: incoming work uses `work-item-to-spec`, AI-enabled work uses `ai-feature-contract`, learning uses `learning-guide`, and implementation review uses `code-review`.
