# Vault Operations

Use these procedures as reusable prompts or operating instructions. Keep scans read-only until the creator selects material to capture.

## Daily scan

**Use when:** The creator wants a current read on approved sources.

1. Read the approved source list and the creator’s current focus.
2. Identify material published since the last scan or within the requested period.
3. Cross-check `inbox/` and `notes/` for duplicates or prior coverage.
4. Score new material `high`, `medium`, `low`, or `skip` for relevance.
5. Present: source, date, linked title, relevance, and a one-line hook.

Do not write capture files during a scan. Prefer relevance over coverage. If a source is unavailable, say what is missing and ask for authorized content instead of guessing.

## Capture to inbox

**Use when:** The creator selects scan results, pastes material, or supplies screenshots.

1. Extract the core claim or finding in one to three sentences.
2. Create the standard capture file in `inbox/` using the source publication date.
3. Add source, canonical URL, dates, and two or three useful tags.
4. Add a short “Why it matters” connection to the creator’s current context.
5. List the files created.

Keep the inbox entry small. It is a retrievable stub, not a deep note.

## Weekly synthesis

**Use when:** Reviewing the last week of captures and changed notes.

Read recent `inbox/` material, changed `notes/`, `ideas/`, `projects/`, and the vault guidance. Write `notes/synthesis/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly.md` with:

1. **Emergent thesis** — A pattern or position forming across the material but not yet stated directly.
2. **Contradictions** — Claims in tension, with links or quotations to both sides.
3. **Knowledge gaps** — Specific missing evidence, source types, or perspectives.
4. **One action** — The single highest-leverage thing to investigate, decide, practice, or build next.

Challenge the material; do not merely summarize it.

## Inbox curation

**Use when:** The inbox is noisy or after synthesis.

For each inbox item, propose one disposition in a table:

- **Promote** — Move durable material into an appropriate `notes/` location and add one to three `[[wikilinks]]`.
- **Keep** — Leave it in `inbox/` because it needs more context or processing.
- **Drop** — Remove stale, redundant, or low-value material.

Wait for approval before moving or deleting anything.

## Context refresh

**Use when:** Current goals, projects, or sources may be stale.

Review recent captures, ideas, and project activity. Propose a small diff to the vault guidance that updates only what the evidence supports: current projects, blockers, next milestones, focus areas, or source lists. Do not write the edits until approved.

## Link or bookmark intake

**Use when:** The creator shares URLs, bookmarks, or screenshots.

Capture supplied content directly. For URL-only items, retrieve content only when authorized and available; otherwise ask for text or screenshots. Preserve the original URL and author or publisher in frontmatter.
